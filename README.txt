THE TORCH ACADEMY WEBSITE
==========================

This is the multi-page version of The Torch Academy website, rebuilt from the supplied original website ZIP.

PAGES
- index.html       Home
- about.html       About Us
- programmes.html  Our Programmes
- learners.html    Who We Serve
- approach.html    Our Learning Approach
- contact.html     Contact Us

ASSETS
- assets/torch-academy-logo.png
- assets/styles.css
- assets/site.js

DEPLOYMENT TO NETLIFY
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder named the-torch-academy-multipage.
3. Confirm that index.html is directly inside that folder, alongside the other HTML pages.
4. Drag the folder (or its contents) into Netlify Drop / your Netlify manual deploy area.
5. Netlify will publish the site. All pages share the same navigation and assets.

IMPORTANT
- The WhatsApp, email and Instagram details have been carried over from the original site. Please check that they are still the exact public contact details you want before sharing the website widely.
- Learner photographs are loaded from Pexels image URLs and therefore require an internet connection when visitors view the site. Pexels identifies these images as free to use under its licence. The photo credits / source pages are listed below.
- The images are illustrative stock photographs, not photographs of The Torch Academy students or staff.
- This is a static website. No registration form or database is required. Enquiries are directed to WhatsApp, email and Instagram.
