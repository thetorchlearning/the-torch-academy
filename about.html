Who we are

Lighting minds. Shaping futures.

The Torch Academy exists to make meaningful academic support more accessible, personal and engaging for learners wherever they are.

Our story

Good learning does more than improve grades.

It helps a child become curious, ask questions, think independently and approach new challenges with greater confidence. We believe every learner deserves the opportunity to experience that kind of growth.

The Torch Academy is an online academic coaching initiative affiliated with Christ Land Schools. While connected to the school’s commitment to education, the Academy has its own online learning focus and delivery approach.

We currently concentrate on Mathematics and Science, offering structured support that responds to each learner’s needs and school expectations.

What guides us

Our purpose and our promise

We want every lesson to move a learner forward, one clear concept at a time.

Our vision

Confident learners, equipped for tomorrow.

To help raise learners who understand what they study, think critically and are prepared to pursue future opportunities with confidence.

Our mission

Make quality academic support personal.

To provide accessible, engaging and curriculum aware online Mathematics and Science coaching that responds to each learner’s needs.

Our philosophy

We teach for understanding.

We believe that progress begins when learners feel able to engage with a subject, make mistakes, ask questions and try again.

01

Start where the learner is

We pay attention to what a learner already understands before deciding what needs to happen next.

02

Make ideas clear

We break down difficult concepts, use examples and give learners room to practise.

03

Build lasting confidence

We want learners to become more independent, not simply dependent on someone giving them answers.
