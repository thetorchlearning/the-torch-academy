Our learning approach

Learning that starts with the learner.

We do not assume that every child learns in the same way or follows the same curriculum. Our approach begins with understanding what the learner needs.

Curriculum aware

Your child’s school programme matters.

A learner in one country, school or year group may be covering different topics from another learner of the same age. That is why we ask parents to share a little information about the child’s learning context.

If available, a copy or link to the school curriculum, scheme of work or current learning outline helps us align lessons with what the child is studying and what is expected next.

  • School name and country
  • Current grade, year or class
  • Subjects and topics being covered
  • Curriculum outline or learning plan, where available
What happens next
1

We listen

We learn about the child, their school context, goals and the areas where support is needed.

2

We understand the starting point

We identify what the learner already knows and where more explanation or practice may help.

3

We shape the lessons

Sessions are planned around the learner’s needs and relevant curriculum expectations.

4

We teach and review

Through live online sessions, practice and feedback, we help the learner make steady progress.
