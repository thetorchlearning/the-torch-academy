What should you tell us?

To help us understand the support your child may need, it is useful to share:

  • Your child’s age and current grade or year
  • The school and country they are studying in
  • The subject or topics they need help with
  • Any learning goals or concerns you have
  • The curriculum outline, if available

You do not need to have all the answers before contacting us. We can take it one step at a time.
