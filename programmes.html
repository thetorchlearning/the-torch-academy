How lessons work

Focused sessions. Active participation.

Lessons take place online through platforms such as Zoom and Google Meet. Learners are encouraged to ask questions, work through examples and participate throughout the session.

We use explanations, guided exercises, discussion and practice to help learners move from simply seeing a solution to understanding how to arrive at it.

  • Live online teaching
  • Guided examples and independent practice
  • Attention to individual learning gaps
  • Progress shaped around learning goals
The learning experience

More than a screen and a lesson.

We want online learning to feel purposeful, interactive and connected to what the learner needs to achieve.
